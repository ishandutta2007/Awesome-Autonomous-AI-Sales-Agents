### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary

Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **autonomous AI sales agents**, **open-source sales automation frameworks**, and **AI SDR platforms**. Whether you are looking for enterprise-grade commercial solutions (such as *Salesforce Sales Agents*, *11x.ai*, and *Artisan*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *OpenCloser*, *SDRbot*, and *Autonomous-sales-agent*), this list covers category leaders, autonomous outreach, and privacy-respecting sales automation.



**Key Market Context:**

- **Salesforce Agentforce for Sales** includes **two core agents**: the **SDR Agent** (works inbound leads 24/7 across channels and languages, qualifying and booking meetings) and the **Sales Coach Agent** (personalized, deal-specific feedback and role-play grounded in CRM data) .

- **Salesforce Agentforce pricing restructured in September 2026**: **Core at $195/user/month** (500K Flex Credits), **Advanced at $395** (1M credits), and **Max at $550** (2.75M credits) — the Max edition delivers **60% more value** than the old Agentforce 1 Edition at no additional cost .

- **11x.ai** builds **Alice (AI Outbound Sales Agent)** and **Julian (AI Phone Agent)**, with **Julian responding in seconds** to demo requests, qualifying, answering questions, and booking meetings instantly across voice, chat, SMS, and WhatsApp .

- **Artisan's Ava 2.0** is the **first AI BDR to run full outbound end-to-end** — identifying and researching prospects, writing 1:1 outreach based on deep research, running email sequences, handling replies including objections, and booking meetings directly on calendars . Deployed across **1,000+ reps** at SumUp, CookUnity, Quora, and Informa .

- **AiSDR** runs a **deeper Salesforce integration than a bidirectional contact sync** — every email, reply, and meeting gets recorded on Contact and Account timelines automatically, with **AI account scoring against 1-4 custom criteria** and **live web research on every prospect** .

- **Regie.ai** orchestrates **plays across channels** with **AI Agents doing the heavy lifting**, automating high-quality outbound at scale so small teams can reach **3–5x more accounts** .

- **Conversica's AnswersIQ** leverages **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** for accurate, consistent, real-time responses across SMS and email, with **fact collection, synonym matching, and dynamic testing** .

- **Reply.io's Jason 4.5** is a **self-learning AI SDR** that watches your edits and turns repeated changes into **AI Learnings** applied automatically — plus **Custom Playbook generation**, **LinkedIn Intent Signals**, and **Calendly booking on autopilot** .

- **Lavender** is an **AI sales email coach** that grades emails and provides tips, with **reply rates increasing by 2-3x** when emails score 90 or above .

- **Outreach's Kaia** is **conversation intelligence built on Outreach's AI Revenue Workflow architecture** — it fuels Omni, Deal Agent, and Research Agent with conversation signals, feeding CRM field updates and deal summaries after every call .

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Salesforce Agentforce for Sales requires Enterprise Edition ($165/user/month) as a minimum** — **Agentforce does not run on Professional Edition, and there is no workaround** . **Analysts warn that bundling reduces line-item visibility and most finance teams are still learning to forecast credit consumption** .

- **Artisan's Ava 2.0 is the first AI BDR to run full outbound end-to-end** — **SOC 2 Type II audited with SSO, RBAC, and guardrails** . **AiSDR runs a deeper Salesforce integration than a bidirectional contact sync** .

- **Open-source AI sales agents are not turnkey** — **OpenCloser requires Node.js, Rust, and Tauri prerequisites** . **SDRbot requires API keys and terminal setup** . **Autonomous-sales-agent requires PostgreSQL, Redis, FastAPI, and multiple API keys** .

- **Always validate outreach quality, deliverability, and CRM data hygiene with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment. Autonomous agents can send thousands of messages — **ensure suppression lists, do-not-contact rules, and compliance guardrails are enforced** before going live. 📈
